# [Opra] Sand Buyer

Buys 10 Buckets of sand and 10 Soda ash per world from the Catherby Trader Crewmember, deposits at the nearby deposit box, hops worlds, and repeats.

## Setup

1. Start near the Catherby charter ship (~2796, 3414) with coins in your inventory.
2. Each round costs 100 coins (10 sand + 10 ash at 5 coins each). Make sure you have enough.
3. Enable the plugin.

## Config

- **Sand target** / **Ash target**: stop after this many items total. 0 = unlimited.
- **World cooldown**: do not revisit a world for this many seconds.
- **Hide overlay**: hide the stats panel.

## Notes

Hops wait until you are idle after depositing, then hop to a normal members world with no skill-total or other requirements. Busy hop rejects retry the same world instead of burning time on a new one.
